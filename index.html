THE GHOST IS A CONNECTION, NOT A VOICE

We find. We don't pretend.

Echoes is a small virtual ofrenda: a place to honor what came before, without pretending that an AI is a person who has passed away.

  1. Keep a story. Add a memory in your own words. The demo stories are fictional.
  2. Choose local AI. Only the load button downloads the open-source model and runtime. The providers see ordinary download metadata such as your IP address. They do not receive your story or query text.
  3. Follow the evidence. The model turns your query and cards into 384-number vectors, compares their cosine similarity, and returns exact source excerpts. Similarity is a ranking signal, not a probability or a fact check.

The model is downloaded from Hugging Face and the runtime from jsDelivr. Your browser may cache those files; future loads may still require network. This page has no analytics, accounts, paid API, or server database. Story cards remain in tab memory unless you opt into this browser's local storage or export a file. Other people using the same browser profile could read locally saved cards.

If AI cannot load, you can keep using the clearly marked keyword search. No generated answers, no impersonation, no hidden AI simulation.
